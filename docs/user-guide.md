# User guide

Paste this into a Zulip announcement (replace the address with your
`PUBLIC_URL`):

```
**Meeting reminders from Google Calendar, right here in Zulip.** Open https://calendar.example.com and click **Sign in with Google** — that's all.
Before each meeting you get a DM from **Calendar** with the time and the video link. Timing, calendars and pause are on the same page under **Settings**.
To pause, DM the Calendar bot **stop** (**start** resumes); **disconnect** deletes your data; **help** lists the commands.
```
